Project 001

Finanzas SV

A bilingual calculator that makes the actual cost of interest-free installment purchases easier to understand.

A sample result from the calculator—not a screenshot of its interface.

Problem

“0% interest” doesn't tell the full story when a purchase has a down payment or an additional fee.

Implementation

Vanilla JavaScript, bilingual content, input validation, and calculations performed in integer cents.

Quality

Automated calculation and translation tests, with builds and deployment handled through GitHub Actions.
